# University branding

The unmodified university seal is sourced from the official Faculty of Medicine website: https://mjekesia.uni-pr.edu/images/logosmall.png

The interface palette uses the seal’s dominant red (#ce2c32), charcoal, gray and white. Darker red (#a51f25) supports text and focus contrast. These colors are sampled from the seal, not asserted to be a published brand specification. Activity-category and availability-status colors remain distinct for readability.
